# Bibliography

This file will contain the bibliography and cited works for *The Luminous Will Ontology*.

The full reference list will be expanded in later releases.

## Key referenced authors and traditions

- Jaegwon Kim — causal exclusion and higher-level causation
- James Woodward — interventionist causation and explanation
- Carl Craver — mechanistic explanation
- Robert Batterman — asymptotic reasoning, universality, and non-causal explanation
- Marc Lange — laws, explanation, and constraint-based reasoning
- John Bell — Bell theorem and quantum nonlocality
